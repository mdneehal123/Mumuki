PDRN serum price in India: what you should pay

PDRN serums in India range from a few hundred rupees to several thousand. Here is what drives the price, and how to know what you are paying for.

By the MUMUKI India team · Updated 4 November 2026

Short answer

In India, PDRN serums from Indian brands mostly cost around ₹550 to ₹1,000 for 30 ml. Imported Korean PDRN serums, ampoules and creams usually cost about ₹1,200 to ₹3,000. The price reflects the full formula, the import, and the seller, not just the PDRN.

Typical PDRN prices in India (2026)

Prices change often with offers; use these as a guide, not a quote.

Why prices differ so much

  • The whole formula: hyaluronic acid, ceramides, collagen and capsules cost more than a simple serum base.
  • Import: imported cosmetics carry shipping, duties, compliance and importer costs.
  • Seller: authorised importers price differently from resellers and grey-market sellers.
  • PDRN source and amount: brands state amounts differently (ppm, %), so compare carefully.

How to avoid overpaying or buying fakes

  • Check for an Indian importer label with name, address, batch number, MRP and expiry.
  • Be wary of prices far below the usual range on marketplaces.
  • Buy from a seller who imports officially into India and gives a GST invoice.
  • Avoid "ship from abroad" offers that cannot handle returns in India.

Is a more expensive PDRN serum better?

Not automatically. Choose by your skin type, the full ingredient list, a texture you will use every day, and an honest seller. A serum you use daily for two months will do more than an expensive one you use once a week.

Frequently asked questions

Why is Korean PDRN serum costly in India?

Imported cosmetics carry international shipping, import duties, compliance and importer costs, on top of the formula itself.

Is a cheap PDRN serum fake?

Not necessarily; many Indian-brand PDRN serums are affordable. But a famous imported product sold far below its usual price is a warning sign.
