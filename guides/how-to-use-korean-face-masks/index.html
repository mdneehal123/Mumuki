How to use sheet, hydrogel and bubble masks: a simple guide

Masks are the easiest way to give skin a quick hydration boost. Here is how to use each type properly, and how often.

By the MUMUKI India team · Updated 4 November 2026

Short answer

Use a sheet mask for 10 to 20 minutes on clean, toned skin, then pat in the leftover essence and seal with a moisturiser. Hydrogel masks are worn as the pack directs; some, like MUMUKI's, are made for longer or overnight wear. Leave-on bubble masks go on after cleansing and absorb in seconds. Most people mask 2 to 3 times a week.

How to use a sheet mask

  1. Cleanse and, if you use one, apply toner.
  2. Unfold the mask and fit it to your eyes, nose and mouth.
  3. Leave it on for 10 to 20 minutes. Do not let it dry out on the face.
  4. Remove it and pat the leftover essence into face and neck.
  5. Seal with a moisturiser.

How to use a hydrogel mask

  1. Cleanse and tone.
  2. Apply the two pieces (top and bottom) smoothly.
  3. Leave it on for the time on the pack. MUMUKI's is made to be worn until it turns transparent, or overnight as the last step.
  4. Remove and pat in any remaining essence.

The brand says MUMUKI's Hydrogel Mask starts turning transparent after about 40 to 50 minutes and recommends around 3 hours of wear.

How to use a bubble mask

  1. After cleansing, pump 2 to 3 times into your palm.
  2. Spread evenly over the face; the bubbles melt in quickly.
  3. Let it absorb for about 30 seconds. Do not rinse.
  4. Follow with your serum and moisturiser.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Leaving a sheet mask on until it dries; it can pull moisture back out.
  • Rinsing off the essence afterwards.
  • Reusing a mask or keeping an opened pouch.
  • Masking on broken, sunburnt or freshly waxed skin.
  • Skipping moisturiser after a sheet mask.

Best times to mask in India

  • The night before a wedding, party or photoshoot.
  • After a long day in the sun (once skin has cooled), or in dry AC rooms.
  • Store sheet masks in a cool place; a few minutes in the fridge feels refreshing in summer.

Frequently asked questions

Can I sleep with a sheet mask on?

No. Sheet masks should come off after 10 to 20 minutes. Hydrogel masks made for overnight use are different.

Should I wash my face after a sheet mask?

No. Pat the leftover essence in, then apply moisturiser.

Can I use a face mask every day?

Gentle hydrating masks can be used often, but 2 to 3 times a week is enough for most people. Leave-on bubble masks are made for daily use.
