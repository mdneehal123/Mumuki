Plant PDRN vs salmon PDRN: what is the difference?

Banana, rose, ginseng: plant "PDRN" is everywhere. Here is how it differs from the original salmon PDRN, and what that means for your skin.

By the MUMUKI India team · Updated 4 November 2026

Short answer

Salmon PDRN is the original, with the most research behind it, mainly from injections. Plant PDRN is made from plant sources and suits people who prefer vegetarian or fish-free skincare, but its research is still early. In a well-made formula both can support hydrated, plumper-looking skin.

The short comparison

Salmon PDRNPlant PDRN (e.g. banana BDRN)
SourcePurified DNA from salmon or troutDNA-based ingredients from plant cells
ResearchLarger body of work, mostly injections and wound healingEarly, mostly lab and brand studies
Fish allergyAvoid or ask a doctorThe PDRN itself has no fish source, but check the full formula
Vegetarian preferenceNoThe PDRN is plant-sourced; other ingredients may not be
Typical useClinic injections, serums, creamsSerums, creams, masks

Why salmon PDRN has more evidence

PDRN was first developed from fish DNA for medical use, so most published studies, including the reviews on wound healing and skin repair, are about salmon or trout PDRN, usually injected (Galeano et al., 2021). Plant versions are newer. In cell studies, some plant PDRN, such as one made from ginseng, acted on the same skin-cell receptor (Molecules, 2023), but there is little or no research on people, and none we know of on banana PDRN.

Why many people still choose plant PDRN

  • Vegetarian preference: the PDRN itself comes from plants, which matters to many Indian families. Other ingredients in the same product may still be animal-derived.
  • Fish allergy: no fish source in the PDRN itself; always check the full ingredient list.
  • Texture and formula: the overall formula (hyaluronic acid, ceramides, collagen, panthenol) matters as much as the PDRN source for hydration and comfort.

How to choose

  • Want the ingredient with the longest research history and have no fish allergy? Salmon PDRN.
  • Prefer vegetarian or fish-free skincare, or have a fish allergy? Plant PDRN, but check every ingredient, not just the PDRN.
  • Either way: check the full ingredient list, patch test, and judge results after 4 to 8 weeks, not 4 days.

Frequently asked questions

Is plant PDRN real PDRN?

Some dermatologists use "PDRN" only for purified fish DNA and call plant versions PDRN-inspired ingredients. Brands use the name for both. What matters for you is the full formula and how your skin tolerates it.

Is banana PDRN vegan?

MUMUKI says its BDRN is banana-derived. Some MUMUKI products also contain honey and hydrolysed collagen, whose source is not stated, so they are not suitable for a strict vegan routine. Message us if you need to check a product.

Is plant PDRN better for sensitive skin?

Not automatically. Sensitive skin reacts to the whole formula. Fragrance-free products and a patch test matter more than the PDRN source.
