Is PDRN safe? Side effects and who should avoid it

PDRN is generally well tolerated in medical studies. Here is who should still be careful, and how to start safely.

By the MUMUKI India team · Updated 4 November 2026

Short answer

PDRN is generally well tolerated. In serums and creams, reactions, when they happen, often come from other ingredients such as fragrance or retinol. Avoid salmon PDRN if you have a fish allergy, ask a doctor in pregnancy or breastfeeding, and patch test any new product for 24 to 48 hours.

How safe is PDRN?

Reviews of PDRN in medical use report very few adverse events across trials and real-world use (Park & Jang, 2025; Galeano et al., 2021).

Who should be careful

  • Fish allergy: avoid salmon-derived PDRN, or ask a doctor. With plant PDRN, still check the full ingredient list for fish-derived ingredients such as collagen.
  • Pregnancy and breastfeeding: there is little safety data on PDRN, and some MUMUKI products list retinol. Ask your doctor before starting.
  • Active skin infection, open wounds or eczema flares: wait until the skin has healed.
  • Very sensitive skin: choose fragrance-free products and patch test first.
  • Injections: only from a qualified doctor, never a salon.

How to patch test

  1. Apply a small amount behind your ear or on your inner arm.
  2. Leave it for 24 to 48 hours.
  3. No redness, itching or swelling? Start on the face once a day.

Reading the label

Check the full ingredient list, the importer or maker's name and address, batch number and expiry. Many reactions come from fragrance or other actives, not PDRN. Frequently asked questions

Is PDRN safe for sensitive skin?

Usually, yes. Choose fragrance-free formulas, patch test, and add one new product at a time.

Can I use PDRN during pregnancy?

There is little safety data on PDRN in pregnancy. Ask your doctor before using it.

Is PDRN vegetarian?

Salmon PDRN comes from fish. Plant PDRN, such as MUMUKI's banana BDRN, is plant-sourced, but other ingredients in the same product, such as collagen, may be animal-derived. Check the full ingredient list.
