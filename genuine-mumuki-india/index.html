Warning signs

  • A price far below what authorised sellers charge.
  • No Indian importer label, or a sticker covering the original details.
  • Expiry date missing, scratched off or very close.
  • Sellers who cannot give an invoice or accept returns for damaged items.
  • "Shipped from abroad" listings with long, untracked delivery times.
