How to use

After cleansing, prep skin with toner. Take the two-piece mask out of the pouch and apply evenly to fit your face. It starts turning transparent after about 40–50 minutes; recommended wear is around 3 hours. Once transparent, remove it and pat in the remaining essence. As a sleeping mask, apply it as the final step of your night routine.

Who it suits

  • Dry or dehydrated skin

  • Skin that feels tired after travel or late nights
