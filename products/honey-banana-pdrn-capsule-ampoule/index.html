How to use

After toner, take an appropriate amount with the dropper and massage the rich, stretchy formula into skin until fully absorbed. It leaves no sticky residue. Tip: the texture is thick, so press the dropper firmly once and release slowly to fill it. Follow with the BDRN Capsule Cream.

Who it suits

  • Dull or tired-looking skin that needs bounce and glow
  • Dry or dehydrated skin, including in AC rooms
  • Oily skin that wants hydration without stickiness
