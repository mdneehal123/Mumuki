Questions

Good to know

Are these products authentic?

Yes. We import directly from Korea. Every product carries an Indian importer label with batch number, MRP and expiry date.

How long does delivery take?

Metro cities take 2–4 working days, and the rest of India 4–7 working days. You'll get a tracking link on WhatsApp and email once your order ships.

Is cash on delivery available?

Yes, COD is available on most pin codes. Prepaid orders (UPI, cards, net banking) ship faster.

Is it suitable for oily skin and Indian summers?

The Capsule Ampoule absorbs quickly with no sticky finish, and the Cream Mist adds light hydration. If you have oily skin, try that combination in summer.

What is your return policy?

If an item arrives damaged or wrong, share an unboxing video within 48 hours and we'll replace it free. Opened skincare can't be returned for hygiene reasons.

Should I patch test?

Always. Apply a small amount behind your ear or on your inner arm for 24 hours before full use, especially if you have sensitive skin.
